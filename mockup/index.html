Balance is not a column

The Rp 2,480,500 on the card is a ledger aggregate cached in Redis, not users.balance. Postgres remains the source of truth; Redis is only the fast path.

The consequence: when the cache and the ledger disagree, the ledger wins, and the difference shows up on the Admin reconciliation screen.

Held balance

Rp 150,000 on hold is a hold from a withdrawal the bank has not finished. The money is already unspendable, but it has not left the system.

This is why three states are needed rather than two: available, held, settled.

Every action is a ledger entry

Top up, transfer, pay, withdraw, refund — not one of them subtracts from a balance. Every one appends two rows that sum to zero.

Even a failed transaction is not deleted. It is reversed with new entries so the audit trail stays whole.

Hot path vs slow path

The home screen and the PIN check must stay under 100 ms, so both are Redis. Transaction history may take 300 ms, so it is Postgres with monthly partitions.

Why this screen must exist

Instant Payout sounds like a product feature, but it is a balance sheet decision. Every second we cut from a merchant's wait is another second our own money is outside the building.

Without a limit and automatic degradation, one fraudulent merchant could drain the float in an afternoon.

The limits we keep

A holdback is insurance, not a punishment. The formula is simple and deliberately explainable to the merchant:

holdback = clamp(1.5%, 45%, expected_loss × 200)

The multiple of 200 is chosen so that collected holdback covers losses roughly two hundred times over. It can come down as data accumulates, and every change is recorded in the audit log.

A score must never come from a model that cannot explain itself. A merchant is entitled to know why their money is held longer.

Why false positives are shown

A fraud rule that is too tight does more damage than no rule at all — legitimate users get locked out, support drowns, and those people move to another app.

That is why every rule has a mode pantau: it is counted and recorded but takes no action, until its false positive rate is low enough to be promoted to active.
